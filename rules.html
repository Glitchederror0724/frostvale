SERVER RULES

Keep FrostVale fair and fun.

These are the public starter rules for the server. Staff may use context when handling situations.

01

No cheating or exploiting

No x-ray, hacked clients, exploits, dupes, or other unfair advantages.

02

No griefing or stealing

Do not destroy, raid, or steal from protected player builds or claims.

03

Keep chat appropriate

No harassment, hate speech, excessive toxicity, spam, or disruptive behavior.

04

No punishment bypassing

Do not evade bans, mutes, warnings, or other staff restrictions using alts or communication-command bypasses.

05

No malicious activity

Do not intentionally crash, lag, exploit, or damage the server or other players' experience.

06

Follow staff instructions

If you disagree with a punishment, use the appeal process instead of arguing or bypassing it.

PUNISHED BY MISTAKE?

Use the official appeal process.
