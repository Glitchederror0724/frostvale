PUNISHMENT APPEALS

Think a punishment was wrong?

Use FrostVale's Discord to submit an appeal. Be honest and include enough information for staff to review what happened.

⚖️

How to appeal

  1. Join the FrostVale Discord.
  2. Open the appropriate ticket or appeal channel.
  3. Include your Minecraft username and punishment details.
  4. Explain why you believe the punishment should be changed.
  5. Wait for staff to review the appeal.
Join FrostVale Discord

Be honest

False information can make an appeal harder to review.

Don't bypass

Do not use alternate accounts or commands to get around a punishment.

Give staff time

Appeals need to be reviewed before a decision is made.
